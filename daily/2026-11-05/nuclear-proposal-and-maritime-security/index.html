Iran Sends a Nuclear Proposal Through Qatar but Keeps Its Seven Conditions

Coverage date: 2026-11-05 · Publication date: 2026-11-13. Historical reconstruction: published on 13 November 2026 using reporting available for the coverage date; it was not originally issued on that date.

Daily brief: A senior Iranian official said Tehran had sent Washington a nuclear proposal via Qatar, while Iran and Oman discussed technical arrangements for safer shipping through Hormuz.

A proposal with a precondition

Reuters reported, citing a senior Iranian official, that Tehran had sent the United States a nuclear proposal through Qatar. The official said Washington was reviewing it but provided no details. Iran’s security chief said Tehran’s seven conditions had to be implemented before negotiations or reopening Hormuz.

The sequencing problem

Washington has said nuclear issues must be part of any initial negotiations. Tehran’s position was that its nuclear proposal would be discussed only after broader conditions were met. The existence of a proposal is evidence of diplomatic activity, not evidence that the parties agree on the agenda or order of concessions.

Oman and the maritime file

Oman’s Foreign Ministry said the Omani and Iranian foreign ministers discussed practical, political and technical arrangements for resuming navigation through Hormuz, including joint mechanisms to protect maritime routes and trade. A technical discussion is meaningful, but it is not yet an agreed shipping regime.
