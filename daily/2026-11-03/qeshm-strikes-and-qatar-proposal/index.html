Qeshm Strikes and a Tanker Attack Put Qatar’s Proposal Under Pressure

Coverage date: 2026-11-03 · Publication date: 2026-11-13. Historical reconstruction: published on 13 November 2026 using reporting available for the coverage date; it was not originally issued on that date.

Daily brief: Limited US strikes on Qeshm, an attack reported against a tanker off Oman and sharply different public positions complicated Qatar’s effort to prevent renewed war.

The military track moves closer

Reuters and CBS reported limited US strikes on Iranian targets on Qeshm Island, described by US officials as defensive action after Iranian fire at a US aircraft or assets. Tehran said it retained the right to self-defence. Accounts differed over the trigger and details; they did not establish a sustained new air campaign.

Shipping remains exposed

UKMTO reported that two unknown projectiles struck a tanker off Oman’s northeast coast. No injuries or environmental impact were reported at the time, and the attacker was not identified. An unattributed incident can still raise insurance costs and deter commercial traffic.

IranSignal assessment

Qatar expected feedback on new ideas within 36 hours, but Iranian officials called the US response unconvincing. The deadline was a diplomatic expectation, not proof that agreement was close. A Hormuz reopening proposal remains vulnerable while ships are attacked or forces use weapons around the strait.
