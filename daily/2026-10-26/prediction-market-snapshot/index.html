IranSignal assessment

The invasion contract is priced above the other five contracts in this snapshot. That comparison does not measure the probability of every kind of attack: its stored rule concerns an offensive intended to establish control over Iranian territory. Air strikes and territorial control are different outcomes. Likewise, leadership recognition, regime change, a nuclear test and possession of a weapon have distinct settlement criteria. Their prices cannot be added to produce a total danger score. The reported daily moves are percentage-point changes, not relative percentage gains. Liquidity, deadlines and market participation can also affect prices. This report preserves the observation rather than converting it into an IranSignal scenario probability. The collection is not exhaustive; a football contract that appeared in the raw feed was excluded as unrelated.

Read each contract’s full rules and deadline before interpreting its quote. No comparable seven-day changes were present in this snapshot.
